Who We Are

About the Club

The UofR Ski & Snowboard Club is a student-run organization open to all University of Regina students and their friends. Whether you're clipping into bindings for the first time or an expert, there's a place for you here.

We organize group trips to local and regional ski hills, coordinate beginner lessons, negotiate club-rate lift tickets, and host social events throughout the season.
