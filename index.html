so who are we?

the uofr ski and board club is a student-run club open to every university of regina student and their friends. first time clipping into bindings or twenty seasons deep — there is room for you here.

we run group trips, sort out beginner lessons, negotiate club-rate lift tickets and throw socials all winter. check our instagram for the newest trips and events.
